About

I am an Architect and Academic with extensive international experience, specializing in the **Decarbonization of the Built Environment** and **Sustainable Systems**. With over 15 years in research, consulting, and teaching, my work focuses on developing and implementing effective strategies to achieve **Net-Zero Emissions** in new and existing buildings. I am passionate about integrating climate policy (like Chile's Climate Change Framework Law) with architectural education, ensuring that future professionals are equipped to lead the industry's response to the climate crisis. My core research revolves around **Whole Building Life Cycle Assessment (WBLCA)**, particularly quantifying embodied carbon in building systems.
